## Overview

In 2020, I started a PC building and resale business, combining my interest in computer hardware with entrepreneurship.

Over the period I operated I built over 100 PCs, never received a negative review and generated more than NZ$25,000 in revenue.

## What I Did

- Sourced new and second-hand PC components.
- Built, configured, and tested desktop computers.
- Diagnosed and resolved hardware and software issues.
- Managed component purchasing, pricing, and sales.
- Communicated directly with customers throughout the sales process.
- Reused components where possible to reduce costs and electronic waste.

## Key Takeaways

Running this business helped me develop practical technical skills alongside an understanding of pricing, cost management, and customer service. It also gave me experience independently managing a business from sourcing components through to completing sales.
